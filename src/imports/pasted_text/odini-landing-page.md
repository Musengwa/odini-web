Design a premium, modern landing page for ODINI Technologies Limited.

ODINI is an AI-powered experience platform that helps people discover, plan, and book unforgettable experiences. It combines AI-powered discovery, intelligent itinerary planning, and seamless booking across Stays, Events, Activities, Restaurants, Cafés, Cultural Experiences, and Local Services.

The website should feel luxurious, minimal, and intelligent, inspired by Apple, Airbnb, Linear, Stripe, Arc Browser, and Notion.

Design style:
• Large typography
• Premium photography
• Plenty of whitespace
• Rounded cards
• Soft shadows
• Minimal glassmorphism
• Smooth scrolling and subtle animations
• Black, white, charcoal, and warm gold accents (#C8A46B)
• Elegant, editorial feel rather than a traditional travel website
